Debre Berhan University is one of the leading comprehensive universities in Ethiopia founded in 2007 E.C. The university has been working hard to achieve its mission of teaching learning, research and community service, and it has continued producing qualified and productive manpower. To this end, the university focuses on academic quality, equity, and fairness.

Debre Berhan University has two campuses: Main campus and Asrat Weldeyes Health Science Campus.

The Online Clearance System for students is a platform designed to simplify the process of obtaining clearance for various purposes, such as end of graduation, withdrwal, end of semester, health problem and more. The system allows students to complete the clearance process conveniently and efficiently.

With the Online Clearance System, students can submit their clearance requests online, eliminating the need for manual paper work and reducing the processing time. The system provides a user-friendly interface where students can track their clearance progress, receive notifications, and view the status of their clearance requests.

Our goal is to streamline the clearance process and enhance the overall experience for students and reducing the over all work load of employee that concern clearance. By providing an online platform, we aim to minimize the hassle and ensure a smooth clearance process, allowing students to focus on their academic and personal endeavors.
